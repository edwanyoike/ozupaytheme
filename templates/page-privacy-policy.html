2.3 Payment data

Payment for licences is processed by WooCommerce and third-party payment gateways. OzuPay does not store full payment card numbers. We receive a transaction record (order ID, amount, status) and retain it for financial compliance purposes.

If you use M-Pesa to pay on this site, your M-Pesa phone number and the transaction reference (MPESA receipt code) are processed by Safaricom's Daraja API and stored against your order record. We do not store M-Pesa PINs or authentication tokens.

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

8. Your Rights Under the DPA 2019

As a data subject under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — You may ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — You may ask us to delete your data where there is no lawful basis for us to retain it.
  • Right to restrict processing — You may ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — You may request a machine-readable copy of data you have provided to us under a contract or consent basis.
  • Right to object — You may object to processing based on legitimate interest, including direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent — Where we process data based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email DPO@ozulabs.com with your name, email address, and a description of your request. We will respond within 21 days as required by the DPA 2019.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

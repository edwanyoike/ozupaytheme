FAQ

Common questions

How does the M-Pesa integration work?+
OzuPay connects your WooCommerce store directly to Safaricom's Daraja API. At checkout the customer receives an STK Push prompt on their phone and confirms with their M-Pesa PIN. The order is then marked paid automatically when the Daraja callback arrives.
Do I need a Safaricom Daraja account?+
Yes. You'll need your own Daraja app credentials (Consumer Key, Consumer Secret and Passkey) plus a Paybill or Till number. OzuPay walks you through entering them, and you can validate everything against the Daraja sandbox before going live. Note that sandbox mode still sends a real STK Push to the test phone, so use a line you control.
What is M-Pesa on Delivery?+
M-Pesa on Delivery collects a configurable deposit via STK Push at checkout. The remaining balance is charged when the customer confirms on delivery: no manual follow-up, no customer chasing.
What is the difference between Free and Pro?+
Free covers STK Push, Paybill & Till, and HPOS compatibility. Pro adds M-Pesa on Delivery with deposits, B2C refunds, analytics, webhooks, QR codes, payment links, POS REST API, Paybill & Till fallback, admin failure alerts, and the Daraja testing panel. There is one Pro licence (KES 5,000 per site, per year) and it includes every Pro feature. Running several sites? Contact us for volume pricing.
Why is Pro billed annually?+
Safaricom updates the Daraja API: credential formats, callback structures and STK flows can change without notice. An active licence keeps your plugin compatible: updates are pushed to your WordPress dashboard automatically and you keep a support channel. A lapsed licence won't break your store, but you stop receiving compatibility patches.
Can I upgrade from Free to Pro later?+
Yes: upgrading keeps all of your existing settings and credentials. Add your licence key in the plugin settings and Pro features are enabled immediately. No reconfiguration required.

Short version: If OzuPay Pro does not work for your WooCommerce store within 30 days of purchase, contact us and we will refund you in full — no questions asked. After 30 days, refunds are assessed on a case-by-case basis.

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

OzuPay offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all Pro Edition licence purchases. If you are not satisfied with the Software for any reason within 30 days of the original purchase date, we will refund your payment in full.

This guarantee reflects our confidence in the product and our commitment to customer satisfaction. We ask only that you give us the opportunity to assist you with any technical issue before requesting a refund, but this is not a requirement.

2. Eligibility

To qualify for a refund under the 30-day guarantee:

  • The refund request must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date.
  • The request must be made by the original purchaser using the email address associated with the order.
  • The purchase must be a first-time purchase of an OzuPay Pro licence. Additional-site purchases are treated as new purchases for this purpose.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • After 30 days — Requests received more than 30 days after the purchase date are outside the guarantee window. We may consider goodwill refunds at our sole discretion in exceptional circumstances.
  • Renewal purchases — Annual licence renewals are non-refundable once the renewed Subscription Period has begun, as the licence key remains active and valid for that period.
  • Incompatibility with unsupported environments — The Software targets WordPress 6.0+ and WooCommerce 7.0+. If your environment falls outside these minimum requirements and the issue stems from your environment rather than the Software, a refund may not be granted. You are encouraged to verify compatibility before purchasing.
  • Misuse or policy violations — Refunds will not be issued where the licence was revoked due to a violation of the Terms of Use.
  • Daraja credential or Safaricom configuration issues — The Software relies on valid Daraja API credentials issued by Safaricom. Issues caused by incorrect credentials, an inactive Safaricom M-Pesa shortcode, or Safaricom account restrictions are outside OzuPay's control and do not qualify for a refund. Our support team will assist you in diagnosing these issues.
  • Feature misunderstanding — Please review the feature list and documentation thoroughly before purchasing. A refund will not be issued solely because a feature you assumed existed is not present, provided the feature was not falsely advertised.

Before requesting a refund: Many payment issues are caused by Daraja configuration — an incorrect passkey, wrong callback URL, or an inactive shortcode. Contact our support team first. Most issues are resolved within 1–2 business days.

4. How to Request a Refund

1

Submit a support ticket

Go to ozupay.com/contact and submit a ticket with the subject "Refund Request". Include your order number or the email address used at checkout.

2

State your reason (optional)

You are not required to provide a reason within the 30-day window. If you are willing to share feedback, we welcome it — it helps us improve the product.

3

Licence deactivation

Upon approval of your refund, your Licence Key will be deactivated. Please remove the Pro Edition from your site(s) after receiving the refund.

4

Refund issued

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within the timeline described in Section 5.

5. Processing Timeline

Once your refund request is approved:

  • OzuPay will initiate the refund within 3 business days of approval.
  • The time for funds to appear in your account depends on your payment provider:
    • M-Pesa: Refunds are processed as a B2C transfer. Funds typically arrive within 24 hours of initiation.
    • Card payments: Funds typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer.
    • Bank transfer: Funds typically arrive within 3–5 business days.

OzuPay is not responsible for delays caused by your bank or payment provider. If a refund has not appeared after 10 business days from our initiation confirmation, contact us at support@ozulabs.com with your refund reference.

6. Renewals

OzuPay does not automatically renew subscriptions. Your licence will continue to function after expiry but will no longer receive updates or support access.

If you choose to renew, the renewal is a new annual purchase governed by the Terms and Refund Policy in effect at the time of renewal. Renewals are eligible for the 30-day money-back guarantee under the same conditions as original purchases.

7. Free Edition

The Free Edition of OzuPay M-Pesa Payments is distributed at no cost. There is no purchase price and therefore no refund applies. Support for the Free Edition is available through the WordPress.org support forums.

8. Partial Refunds and Credits

In situations outside the 30-day window where a partial refund or account credit may be appropriate (for example, a confirmed software defect that substantially impairs the Software's core function and cannot be resolved), OzuPay may, at its sole discretion, offer a partial refund or account credit. These are not guaranteed and will be assessed individually.

Account credits, where issued, can be applied to a future purchase and are valid for 12 months from the date of issue.
